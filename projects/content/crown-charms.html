Crown Charms

Turning jewellery into a digital shopping experience

Overview

Crown Charms is a jewellery e-commerce concept aiming to make products feel elegant online. The design focuses on clean product presentation, elegant typography, minimal navigation, and a premium brand atmosphere.

The Client's Challenge

Customers need to feel confident when buying jewellery online; the site must present products as premium while keeping navigation simple.

What I Solved

I built a minimalist product-first interface with clear CTAs, responsive layouts, and refined visual identity.
