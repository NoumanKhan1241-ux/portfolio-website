FlamLingo

Making Pashto learning more accessible through technology

Gallery

Overview

FlamLingo is an interactive language-learning platform created to make learning Pashto more accessible through technology. It combines structured lessons, quizzes, progress tracking, pronunciation practice, translation, and conversational learning.

The Problem

Learners of regional languages often have limited access to modern digital learning options. FlamLingo addresses limited resources, lack of structured lessons, pronunciation practice, and opportunities for conversation.

What I Built

Core features include user registration, interactive lessons, quizzes, progress tracking, an AI conversation partner, pronunciation analysis, and course management.

Technical Challenges

Challenges included authentication, database relationships, AI integration, pronunciation analysis, and payment workflows. Each required careful design, testing, and iteration.

Result

FlamLingo became an interactive platform focused on helping users learn Pashto through structured content, assessments, and AI-powered conversational practice.
