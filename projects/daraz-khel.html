Overview

Daraz Khel Construction Company needed a professional digital presence that could represent the quality and range of its construction services. The goal was to move beyond a basic company introduction and create a website that communicates trust, professionalism, and capability to potential clients.

The Client's Challenge

Before the website, the company had limited ability to present its services and capabilities online in a structured and professional way.

What I Solved

I created a professional website structure that makes the company easier to understand within a few seconds. The solution included a strong homepage introduction, clear presentation of services, responsive layout, and strong CTAs.

How I Did It

I structured the content around a simple user journey: Discover → Understand → Trust → Contact, focusing on typography, spacing, images, navigation and mobile responsiveness.
