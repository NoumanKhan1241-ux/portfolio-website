About Me

I'm a passionate Full Stack Developer who believes that technology is more than just code. It is a way to turn ideas into something real, useful, and meaningful. My journey in computer science has taught me that every great solution begins with curiosity, patience, and the courage to keep going when things don’t work the first time.

I enjoy building digital experiences that combine clean design, thoughtful functionality, and powerful technology. For me, development is not simply about making an application work; it is about understanding the problem, imagining a better solution, and creating something that can genuinely make a difference.

Every challenge I face teaches me something new. Every bug, failed attempt, and difficult problem has helped shape the developer I am becoming. I believe growth comes from staying curious, learning continuously, and never being afraid to step outside my comfort zone.
